Stepwell helps you understand what research says about lifestyle and cancer: whether you want to lower your risk, are going through treatment, are living well after cancer or are caring for someone.

ASK AND READ
• Ask questions in your own words. Answers come only from a curated library of research and guidelines.
• Every statement links to the passage it comes from and shows how strong the evidence is.
• Read short insights on physical activity, alcohol, smoking, nutrition, sleep, fatigue, screening, fear of recurrence and more, for your situation.

TAKE SMALL STEPS
• Build a personal plan from the insights and tick off what you did each week.
• Take a lifestyle check that compares your habits with public recommendations. It is not a risk score.
• Prepare questions for your next appointment and take notes during the visit.
• Optional weekly reminders, read-aloud, offline reading and step counts.

SUPPORT EACH OTHER
• A small, moderated community for encouragement: pseudonymous, members only, and every post is checked before it appears.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account needed. Your profile, plan and lifestyle check stay on your device.
• No advertising, no tracking, no sale of data.
• Optional end-to-end-encrypted sync between your devices.

In English, Français, Español, Português, Deutsch, Italiano and العربية.

IMPORTANT
Stepwell provides general health information. It does not diagnose or treat, and it does not replace your care team. In an emergency, call 112 in Europe or your local emergency number.
